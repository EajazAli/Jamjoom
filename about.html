The company

Tegan Al Fateh Factory Co. Ltd. (Jamjoom Printing & Packaging — JP&P) is a newly established manufacturer of Duplex Paper Board based inner folding cartons.

In-house from day one

JP&P commenced operations with an in-house Graphic Arts Design Center, AutoCAD, Offset Printing both UV & Conventional, and Die cutting & Folding gluing machines.

We own and operate our key machineries that are state of the art with cutting edge technology. This means we are able to provide you with the product exactly as per your required specification at a highly competitive price.
