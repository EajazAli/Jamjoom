Color reproduction has a fundamental problem: a given “color” doesn’t necessarily produce the same color in all process.

Color variation across production is a major challenge for the packaging industry. Only a properly color-managed workflow ensures accurate color throughout design, proofing and printing.

What we have

  • JP&P standardized its pre-press, proof and printing processes as per ISO 12647-2 standards for offset packaging.
  • We invested in advanced color management modules from GMG Color (Germany) and X-Rite / Pantone (USA).
  • Our QA, prepress and printing departments are trained for continuous monitoring, with standard operating procedures and quality checks.
  • All processes are equipped with state of the art software and hardware technologies.

Color affects every workflow, so every workflow needs process standardization and color management.

JP&P performs process standardization and calibration of display monitors, proofing printers and offset printing presses to ISO 39 standards, with regular cross-checks and corrective actions. Board profiles are created using the automated X-Rite i1iO Pro 3 spectrophotometer to match color according to board changes. Press standardization and board profiling occur every six months, adjusting for board and chemical changes to ensure consistent color output.

Our work flow

What we offer

  • JP&P Standardized Pre-press, Digital Hard Proof, CTP and Press as per ISO 12647-2 workflow which can print ISO 39, Gracol or Customized Standards.

  • JP&P bring consistent print results across print runs with X-Rite/Pantone Intellitrax Scanning System and confirm prints are run as per ISO 39 or Gracol Standards.

  • Digital hard proofs printed with different paper simulations — folding box board, duplex board, kraft board, grey board — within delta E 3.

  • Best achievable quality on different packaging substrates by simulating ISO 39 coated paper results, with every job compared against its digital proof.

  • JP&P can print CMYK & Spot Colors within delta E less than 3 and ensure TVI’s & Gray Balance is within strict ISO 12647-2 tolerance.

  • X-Rite Ink Formulation Software ensures spot colors are mixed within Delta E 2.

  • Global vision inspection technology is used for print and artwork comparison, to verify the integrity of every design element.

  • An expert printing team trained to use color management tools for accurate color reproduction.
