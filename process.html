JP&P customer journey

Six steps · first call to final walk-through

A disciplined, thorough process ensures creativity becomes reality. That’s why we cover every step and detail, from the first call to the final walk-through.

01

Understanding your Goals and Requirements

At JP&P we believe every project is unique. We begin by understanding your requirements — analyzing the products, compliance, and the end customer’s need — to define the success of the project.

02

Proposal

We strive for outstanding partnership with our customer by providing the maximum quality products with a competitive price in a minimum lead time of delivery.

03

Social Responsibilities

We support the community and environment in the markets where we operate.

04

Our Strength

JP&P is focused on a specific segment: we specialize in the Pharmaceutical Boxes and Food Boxes industries, and have invested in professional human capital and the latest available technology. JP&P follows Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP).

05

Quality Inspections

JP&P sets itself apart from our competitors by prioritizing the High-Quality Standards of our products. We established the best Quality Management System (QMS) to meet our Customer Quality Standards.

06

Service

JP&P strongly believes in and practices the On-Time In-Full (OTIF) process to meet delivery dates with customers.
