JP&P packaging services

First in the region, built on our own machines.

Tegan Al Fateh Factory Co. Ltd. (Jamjoom Printing & Packaging — JP&P) is a newly established first manufacturer of Duplex Paper Board based inner folding cartons in the region.

We own and operate our key machineries that are state of the art with cutting edge technology. This means we are able to provide you with the product exactly as per your required specification at a highly competitive price.
